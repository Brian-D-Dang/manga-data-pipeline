## Assistant

A reasonable fiducial value is

\[
U \approx 6.7\times10^{-4},\qquad \log_{10}U\approx-3.18.
\]

This uses the paper’s representative H II-region value \(q=2\times10^7\ \mathrm{cm\,s^{-1}}\) and \(U=q/c\).

However, “Solar-System-like” mainly implies roughly solar metallicity; it does not uniquely determine \(U\). The paper’s solar-metallicity models span approximately \(1.7\times10^{-4}\le U\le10^{-2}\). The notebook calculates metallicity from [N II]/[O II], but lacks [O III]/[O II], so it cannot independently determine \(U\). Also, its metallicity equation should use `np.log10`, not `np.log`.
