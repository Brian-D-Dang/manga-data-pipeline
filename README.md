# manga-data-pipeline

<p>The manga-data-pipieline takes data cubes for the MaNGA (**Ma**pped **N**earby **G**alaxies at **A**PO) survey and transforms them into maps to show metal rich areas on a galaxy. Along with maps of radiation, one can have a map of habitable zones across a galaxy.</p>
<p>We take the flux data of MaNGA data cubes and slice it along wavelengths corresponding to spectral emission lines. With Kewley and Dopita's paper (name and year of paper here), we use a diagnostic to generate a map of metallicty and ionization parameter.</p>
